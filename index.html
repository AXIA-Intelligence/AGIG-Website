Practical questions

The questions worth asking before you proceed.

Why not just use a general AI chat tool?

A general tool can research, draft, and answer, and skilled prompting makes those answers better. The gap is everything it starts without.

This begins every conversation already holding your world. Your decisions, commitments, relationships, and history are present from the first line, and the system understands what it holds well enough to bring the right piece forward rather than leave you to search for it. You say what needs to happen, and it works out how to get there. What comes back does not rise and fall with your wording, because the discipline is built into the system rather than supplied by you.

The rules are yours, set once. The permissions, priorities, and standards you define are governed at the account level and hold across every conversation, not one chat at a time. The model itself is one mechanism inside this. It is not the system, and it is not the authority. That is the difference between a tool you prompt and infrastructure that carries your world.

Why not hire an assistant?

Hire the assistant. This does not replace one, and the two are stronger together. A good assistant brings judgment, relationships, and hands in the real world that no system provides. What a person cannot do is hold the full record of everything you are accountable for, across every domain, without gaps, year after year. People carry a great deal, and then they forget, get overloaded, or move on. The system is the continuity underneath all of it.

It also makes an assistant more effective, not redundant. You use it to shape and track what you hand off, so a request is clear, the reasoning travels with it, and nothing quietly drops. The assistant works against a shared record instead of a scattered thread of messages, which keeps the work accountable. When you are ready, the assistant can work inside the same system with access you scope, so what they produce flows back into your record. The point is not fewer people. It is more capacity, with the thread held.

How much work will this require from me?

Less than you would guess, and most of it is at the start. Onboarding is done in under a day. There is a short learning curve, shaped by how much you have worked with tools like this before, and what flattens it is simple. You talk to the system the way you would talk to a capable person, in plain language. There is nothing to memorize.

You are not left to find your way alone. While the system is being built around you, you have a direct line to the founder and a weekly check-in, so progress stays visible and every question has a place to land. Your ongoing part is only what genuinely needs you: setting priorities, approving what the system can reach, correcting it when it is wrong, and signing off on anything consequential. Avant Garde carries the setup, the connections, and the refinement. As your life surfaces something worth handling properly, we build for it then, rather than forcing every case into the first day.

What information does the system access?

Only sources you approve and that are appropriate to the purpose. The system may connect broadly across your operating world, but permissions remain explicit and minimum-necessary at the account, folder, database, workflow, and action level. Some domains may remain separate where privacy, professional authority, ethics, security, or preference requires it.

What happens when models or providers change?

Your source record, governance, permissions, working rules, decisions, and review standards remain separate from any one model. Models and interfaces can be reassessed or replaced without surrendering the underlying continuity layer.
