About the founder

Travis Lewis

From mission-critical trading infrastructure to Generative Governed Intelligence built around people whose decisions, commitments, and responsibilities span consequential parts of life and work.

Problems where the answer has to be right

I have always been drawn to problems where the answer has to be right, not merely persuasive. That started early, with mathematics at Choate and later at the University of Pennsylvania. Numbers do not negotiate. A system either works or it does not.

Systems that have to work

After school, I built my career in mission-critical technology. Trading desks where failure was expensive and time mattered. My job was to understand the operating requirement, translate it into architecture that could survive pressure, and keep it reliable when money was at risk.

That taught me something I still use: the purpose of any system is to serve the outcome. The person accountable for the outcome needs to trust what the system gives back.

From infrastructure to decision systems

I eventually left that world and founded Fulcrum Solutions, where I built computational infrastructure for cryptocurrency mining. Different domain, same discipline: design a system that works under real conditions, monitor it, and improve it.

From there I became a professional trader. And that is where I discovered the single weakest component in any strategy: human emotion. Attachment to outcomes. The conviction that you are right when the data says otherwise. Discipline fails at the point where it matters most, and it fails because of the person, not the method.

So I wrote my trading philosophy into code and took myself out of the loop. The algorithm executed what I had designed, without the emotional interference. It made money.

The humbling

When Artificial Intelligence matured enough to analyze my algorithm, I fed it in. It rewrote the strategy and improved it. A system I had spent years refining was made better by a machine in a fraction of the time.

I could have been threatened by that. Instead I chose to understand the capability completely. I spent hundreds of hours studying how language models reason, where they fabricate, what they need to perform well, and what they need to stay responsible.

What surprised me most was discovering that this project, augmenting human judgment with technology, was not new at all. It was a century-old tradition. Intelligence Augmentation, where the human stays the hero. I was standing on the shoulders of giants, and almost no one talks about it.

The reconciliation

Here is the reconciliation I had to make. In pure-pattern trading, removing the human wins. The emotion is noise, and the algorithm is better without it.

But consequential life decisions are not pure-pattern problems. They involve values, relationships, history, ambiguity, and judgment that no machine can replace. In those decisions, the system’s job is to remove the noise so your judgment can be at its best. It does not replace you. It prepares you.

Why Avant Garde exists

Avant Garde grew from that understanding. I build governed intelligence systems for people whose responsibilities span decisions, commitments, relationships, and time. The tradition beneath the work is Intelligence Augmentation, a 130-year lineage where the human stays the hero.

The infrastructure may connect the major systems early. The deeper value develops through use: the system earns trust, you become more skilled at directing it, better practices emerge, and both judgment and capability grow stronger over time.

The system prepares the record, preserves continuity, and strengthens the person. The decision stays yours.

They sell you the tool. We install the system.

“I want to be a catalyst for the people making the world better, by helping them make better decisions.”

Travis Lewis

Founder and Chief Cognitive Architect · Avant Garde International Group
