Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you decide.

I already use AI. What is different here?

A chat tool answers from the context you give it. Avant Garde builds the governed system around the work: relevant history, rules, standards, decisions, and validated learning stay available so you do not rebuild the foundation every time.

What happens when a better AI model comes along?

There will always be a newer model. What matters is what you own underneath it. Your memory, governance, standards, and validated learning stay with the system, so new capability does not require starting over.

Won’t AI take over my decisions?

No. The system can prepare, research, organize, and surface trade-offs. You define the objective, set the boundaries, and make the consequential calls. Judgment stays yours.

Why not hire an assistant?

Hire one if you need one. A good assistant brings judgment, relationships, and real-world execution. The system provides continuity underneath the work: context, decisions, standards, and open commitments stay available, making delegation clearer and follow-through easier. They are complementary, not substitutes.

Will this become another system I have to manage?

That is what the design is meant to avoid. Where practical, the system works through tools you already use. You learn it through real work rather than administering another platform. Your ongoing role is setting priorities, approving access, correcting what is wrong, and making the decisions that require you.

What information can the system access?

Only sources you approve and that are appropriate to the purpose. Access is permissioned and can be changed or revoked. Some information may remain intentionally separate when privacy, security, professional authority, ethics, or personal preference requires it.

What am I actually buying?

A finished, working system built around the way you work. It includes the governance and memory underneath it, training to use it, proven workflows adapted to your needs, and the installation and validation required to make it useful. You own the data. You own the decisions. The AI is the leverage.
